Industry internship, June to July 2026

An 8 week UX design internship at Planefold, taking a construction toy system through product positioning, packaging direction, and a website redesign.

Overview

Snapbits was one product turning into a product family, and the website had to catch up.

Planefold is a design led toy company. Their first product, Snapbits, blends construction with puzzles, built from magnetic 3D blocks inspired by the Soma puzzle. During my 8 weeks there, Snapbits was evolving from a single existing set into a broader family with a new, younger line. My work spanned understanding the market, defining how the two lines should be positioned, shaping packaging and activity content, and redesigning the website end to end in Figma.

Due to confidentiality, I’m unable to share the full project details, but the experience gave me valuable insight into connecting product strategy, brand communication, and UX/UI design.
